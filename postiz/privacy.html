Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how our social media scheduling application ("Postiz") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including social media account credentials and content you schedule through our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to operate and improve our services, including scheduling and publishing content to your connected social media accounts.

3. Social Media Platforms

When you connect social media accounts (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube), we access only the permissions necessary to schedule and publish content on your behalf.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored securely on our private servers. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. TikTok Data

When connecting TikTok, we request access to post videos and read basic account information. We do not store TikTok user data beyond what is necessary for scheduling.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us at: admin@admin.com
